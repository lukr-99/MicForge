MicForge Crafting preview sample: voice-sample.wav
==================================================

This clip is a trimmed excerpt (~11 seconds, downconverted to 16-bit mono)
of a public speech-corpus recording, used as the default "sample voice" for
previewing effects on the Crafting tab.

Source:  "Speech corpus - example of raw audio: HARVARD list 01.wav"
Author:  Philippa Demonte (ORCID 0000-0001-5810-2737), University of Salford
DOI:     https://doi.org/10.17866/rd.salford.7857770.v1
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
         (CC BY-NC 4.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/

Citation:
  Demonte, Philippa (2019). Speech corpus - example of raw audio:
  HARVARD list 01.wav. University of Salford. Media.
  https://doi.org/10.17866/rd.salford.7857770.v1

The CC BY-NC 4.0 license permits noncommercial use with attribution, which
matches MicForge's own PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 license. Only a short
excerpt is included, purely as a built-in test/preview signal.

To add your own preview voices, use "Add…" on the Crafting tab, or drop WAV
files into:
  %AppData%\MicForge\samples\
